Privacy & terms

How StudentStay uses your information, and the rules for using the website.

What we collect

When you send a message or create an owner account we collect your name, email address, phone number and the details you provide.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, connect students with providers and run your account. We do not sell your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing immisumah01@gmail.com.

Listings

Property owners are responsible for making sure their listing details, prices and photos are accurate and up to date. Our team checks every listing before it goes live.

Agreements

Rental agreements are made directly between students and providers. Always view a property and get a written agreement before paying.

Acceptable use

Do not post false, misleading or offensive content. We may remove listings that break these terms.
