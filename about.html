About StudentStay

StudentStay helps students find safe, affordable and convenient accommodation near their university. We make it easy to search, compare and contact accommodation providers, all in one place.

We started because finding a room in Freetown meant scrolling through WhatsApp groups, social media posts and word of mouth, with no clear prices or photos. StudentStay puts that information in one organised place, for students and for the owners who rent to them.

How it works

    Find accommodation that suits your needs and budget.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Contact

    Get in touch with providers directly and request a room.
