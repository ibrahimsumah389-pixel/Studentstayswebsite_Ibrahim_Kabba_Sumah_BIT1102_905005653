Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to use StudentStay?

No. Searching, saving and contacting providers is free for students. Listing a property is free during our launch period.

Are the listings checked?

Yes. Our team checks the details and photos of every new listing before it goes live.

How do I pay rent?

You pay rent directly to the provider, as set out in your rental agreement. StudentStay does not collect rent. Always view the room and get a written agreement before you pay anything.

What happens to my personal information?

We only use your details to reply to you and to connect you with providers. We never sell your information. Read our Privacy & terms for the full details.
